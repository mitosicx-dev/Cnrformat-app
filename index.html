PHASE 3 · MATHEMATICAL CONSISTENCY

Optimization Lab BETA

Tag card roles, calculate exact hand probabilities, sample reproducible draws, and compare saved builds.

What the Lab measures

Statistics are based on roles you tag manually. A “starter” means a card you have verified can start a relevant line. These are hand-draw statistics—not effect-resolution, disruption, combo, or match simulations. CNR empty-hand Draw Phase rules are not modeled here.

1 · Define card roles

Click a role for each Main Deck card. Multiple roles per card are supported; Extra Deck cards aren't sampled.

★ Starter✦ Extender◼ Potential brick⚡ Board breaker◎ Disruption

2 · Analyze opening hands

Choose 5 opening cards or 6 cards seen after a normal draw. Probability calculations are exact; simulated draws are random samples.

3 · Sensitivity test

Hypothetically swap non-starter copies for designated starters without changing deck size. This does not recommend specific cards or validate the replacements.

4 · Compare deck versions

Compare the current draft against a saved deck or two saved versions. Only role-based consistency metrics are compared—not win rate.

Evidence policy

CALCULATED = exact probability from the entered ratios. SAMPLED = reproducible random hands. USER-TAGGED = your card role assumptions. NOT TESTED = legal combo sequences, interruptions, opponent interactions and win rates. Never present this Lab's output as a proven competitive ranking.

PHASE 4 · COMPETITIVE TESTING

Competition Center BETA

Create best-of-3 matches or full round robins, deal opening hands, record actual duels and rank decks from real results.

Evidence first

This app does not automatically play Yu-Gi-Oh! or resolve card effects. Match records come from games you play or verify externally. Opening-hand samples are not AI duels. Recorded win percentages are descriptive statistics, not predictions.

RESTRICTION POLICY

Strictest rule always wins

Official TCG prohibition overrides a CNR copy allowance. Category exceptions only exempt that CNR category ban—not an official or named ban. This ruleset is a fixed snapshot dated October 21, 2026.
